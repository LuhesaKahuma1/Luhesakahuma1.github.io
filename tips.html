SMALL STEPS, STRONGER SECURITY

Free cybersecurity tips

Simple, defensive habits can help protect your accounts, devices, and personal information.

Protect your accounts

  • Use a different, strong password for each important account.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication wherever available.
  • Never share verification codes or passwords in response to unexpected messages.
  • Use a password manager to create and store unique passwords.

Spot suspicious messages

  • Pause before opening unexpected links or attachments.
  • Verify urgent requests through a separate, trusted channel.
  • Be cautious of messages pressuring you to act quickly or reveal private information.
  • Report suspicious messages using the service’s official reporting options.

Keep devices safer

  • Install operating system and app updates from official sources.
  • Back up important files regularly and check that you can restore them.
  • Use a screen lock and avoid leaving devices unattended while signed in.
  • On public Wi-Fi, avoid sensitive tasks unless you can use a trusted, secure connection.
